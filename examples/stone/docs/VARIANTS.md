## The two concepts

### v1: "WHAT'S IT WORTH?": Rai's live TV show from the bottom of the sea

Rai hosts a glittering variety show from a studio built out of a shipwreck on the seabed. Her audience is fish, crabs
and other sunken things, and the crew are crabs and an octopus. The show's segments tell the story:
- archive flashbacks;
- a shopping channel;
- a rich list;
- the audience vote;
- a pitch round;
- a quick-fire round;
- an outside broadcast "up the road";
- a dating segment;
- a panel debate;
- a studio-audience lift.

The ledger is the studio's giant LED scoreboard. It shows the woman's score: 0. In the breakdown the audience comes
down to carry the pole, and the whole stage rises out of the sea onto the island's beach for a fireworks finale. The
last shot pulls back from a TV in the woman's living room. She is asleep on the sofa with her child, and the credits
roll on the screen.

Its nuance:
- satire and showbiz, sassy, fourth-wall winks;
- multi-camera TV grammar: crash zooms, a tally light, lower thirds, a channel-flip wipe, a night-vision feed;
- stage-light colour (pink, yellow and cyan spots on deep indigo, gold bulbs);
- the lyric as the show's on-screen graphics.

### v2: "THE DIVER": a girl finds the stone

At dusk a girl in a mask and snorkel dives off a jetty with a torch. She is the daughter of the woman awake at four.
- **The discovery:** her beam sweeps past Rai. "You can't see me": Rai is caught in the light and grins.
- **The story:** Rai tells it by projecting it from the hole in her heart onto the dark water like a magic lantern.
- **The ride:** they ride a manta over the reef to the trader's rusting iron wreck.
- **Home:** the girl surfaces into the town at night and goes home. At four in the morning she is the child with the
  fever, and her mother sits awake beside her.
- **The ledger:** a living ledger book with an eye on its cover. It looks, blinks and writes 0.
- **Pre-chorus 2:** told through the girl's toy doll's house.
- **Chorus 2:** a fever dream in which the bedroom floods gently into the sea.
- **The bridge:** a family dinner argument round a round table. The table rises as they all stand: every one of them
  is carrying it.
- **The breakdown:** at night she leads the village to the shore with lanterns. Divers thread the pole and the ropes.
- **The final chorus:** Rai breaks the surface at dawn and is carried up the beach to the girl's house. The girl hands
  her mother a slate with the night's real numbers on it.
- **The outro:** years later, the grown girl's wedding, with the stone in flowers and the mother, older, in the front
  row.

Its nuance:
- story and tenderness, a partner for Rai to act with (wow, soft, protective, fierce, joyous);
- cinematic grammar: caustics, god rays, focus pulls, slow push-ins, a dream ripple;
- natural light that turns through the day (dusk, night, 4 am, a dream, evening lamp, night lanterns, dawn, a bright
  wedding day);
- the lyric rising into place like bubbles.
