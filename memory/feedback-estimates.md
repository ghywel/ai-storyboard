Measure a representative slice (perf on the busiest shot, a short segment) and multiply, instead of guessing.

**Why:** an early estimate put a 4K60 final with motion blur at 4-6 hours; measured, a 3.5-minute film took about
30-45 minutes on a laptop.

**How to apply:** quote estimates with the measurement they come from.
