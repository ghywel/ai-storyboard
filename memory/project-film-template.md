Copy and fill for each film:

- **The brief** (date, the director's words where possible).
- **Decisions** made by the director, in their words, dated (take choice, credit form, concept approvals, notes).
- **Phase now**: script / kit / plates (which done) / gates / check render / final / delivered.
- **Where things are**: treatment, guide, timeline, takes, renders, deliveries.
- **DECISION OWED**: anything waiting on the director, with a recommendation.
- **Next**: the one next step.

Update it at every milestone; a new session reads it first.
