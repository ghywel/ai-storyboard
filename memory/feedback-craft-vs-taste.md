Sort every standing note into craft (holds for any film: readable lyric, real places with clues, silhouettes that
emote, restraint, gates, measuring) and taste (one film's look and register: neon, slams, anime acting, chibi, comedy).
Taste carries to the same song's other takes ("perfect, no notes" is a direction). For a new song, ask at phase 0 which
taste, if any, the new film keeps.

**Why:** the method was written from one film, and its memory stated that film's taste as rules. A second song with a
different tone (Agnosto Theo, 2026-10-05: epic and reverent) would have inherited chibi pops and manga marks.

**How to apply:** DIRECTION.md marks taste as "Taste (the Stone films)". In a new film's scene guide, list what carries
over and what does not, at the top of THE DIRECTION.
