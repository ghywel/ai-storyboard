Body language (slump, hands to face, hug, cheer, lean in a doorway), staging (who looks at whom, who stays still) and
small emote signs (a tear, a sigh, a heart, zzz). Each recurring person keeps one accessory (a cap, a plait and shawl,
a bobble hat) so they read as the same person across plates.

**Why:** the director's note: "a tear drop down a cheek. Mum reading the bedtime story but dad watching at the door."

**How to apply:** put the cast in the shared kit with their accessories, so every author draws the same people.
