Use the credit form the human chooses, exactly. Credit the music generator and any engine built on. Never put a
third-party image, recording or text of unknown provenance into a film or a public repository. Before publishing,
sweep the files for private names, e-mail addresses, home paths and account names.

**Why:** a film's end card and a public repository outlive the session.

**How to apply:** keep the credit text in the treatment, check the rendered credits frame at full size, and run the
sweep before every public commit.
