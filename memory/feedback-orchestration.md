Build the kit (rig, sets, cast, graphics, lyric presenter), look-test it, commit and freeze it. Then split the
timeline into groups of two or three adjacent plates, one author each, briefed by the scene guide. Authors never edit
the kit; they write helpers in their own files and report gaps, which the orchestrator folds back afterwards.

**Why:** a kit changing under parallel authors breaks them silently. Two authors once had to agree which plate gives
a story prop; messages crossed and neither gave it.

**How to apply:** settle every cross-plate decision in the guide's continuity table before work starts; review each
contact sheet as it arrives; check joins on a cuts sheet; commit after each review so an interruption (crash, usage
limit) costs nothing. Interrupted agents can be resumed by message; their files are on disk.
