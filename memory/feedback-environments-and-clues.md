No gradient slides. Build places with back, mid and front layers, light sources and something alive. Plant in each shot
a background detail that is a clue to the scene or foreshadows the next, and write in the script what it means.

**Why:** the director's note: "background detail is important, and like any good director, seemingly irrelevant
background details are symbolic clues to what is going on in the scene or foreshadowing for what is to come".

**How to apply:** keep a continuity table in the scene guide (each recurring prop and its state per plate); ask each
author to list their clues and meanings in the report; graphic shots (a slam on a sunburst) are punctuation only.
