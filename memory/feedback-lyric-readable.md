---
name: feedback-lyric-readable
description: "Every sung line is on screen, per word, never ahead of the voice, never covered, presented inside the concept"
metadata:
  type: feedback
---

Word-by-word karaoke from the aligned times (dim up to 0.4 s early; a word lights at its start); a dark band when the
picture is busy; title-safe; balanced rows with no orphans; in back-to-back rapped lines hold each line until its last
word has filled. Present it inside the concept (lower thirds for a TV show, rising bubbles under water); slams go on
top, never instead.

**Why:** a lyric that drops its rhyme word or hides behind a glow breaks the film's contract with the song.

**How to apply:** one lyric presenter in the kit; clear the additive glow layer under it; check a frame of every line
on a contact sheet.
