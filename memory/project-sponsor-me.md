- **The brief (19 October 2026, Gareth).** An emotive plea for World Vision's child sponsorship, given to the
  charity for its social media. Devastating, it starts from what children endure and connects viewers with their guilt for
  standing by, then builds to a future where no child is left behind and colour and country mean nothing: desolation
  to the welcome of God's kingdom. Christian, it challenges the non-religious without alienating them. Not about Gareth's
  family. Inspiration: a slow live worship song (sing-spoken verses, a soaring chorus, a metronome kick, a guitar that
  builds).
- **Decisions made.** None yet beyond the brief.
- **Phase now.** Words and design written (`songs/sponsor-me.md`, `films/sponsor-me/sponsor-me-design.md`).
  Waiting on Suno takes, the reference MP3 and ChatGPT look stills.
- **Standing rules.** The girl is invented and never the child Gareth's family sponsors. Dignity: hardship through
  things and distances, never her body. The guilt is on the viewer's side. No saviour. Christian in its own voice, and help
  never depends on faith. Only promises a new sponsor can keep (A4 padded envelope, money to the community, 12-week
  first letter, no home visits). Painted, never photographic. The charity's name and logo only with their agreement.
- **DECISION OWED.** Lead voice (rec. British male), words on screen (rec. yes, in the concept), naming the country
  (rec. no), contacting World Vision UK (rec. before publishing; two end cards), lengths (rec. the full film plus 60/30/15 s
  cutdowns), the look (stills), credits, gates (rec. stop at the boards). See the design document, section 14.
- **Next.** Measure the chosen take; write LOOK.md from the stills; build the felt-tip and crayon medium.
