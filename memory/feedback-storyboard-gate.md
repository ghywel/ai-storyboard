name: feedback-storyboard-gate
description: "Big visual work gets a storyboard review (keyframe + line + idea per shot) before polish and before any final render"
metadata:
  type: feedback
---

Block every shot in at storyboard depth (readable, on the beat), then show the director one keyframe per shot with its
line and a one-sentence idea. Polish only after their notes. Then a 1080p preview with the music, then the final.

**Why:** in code-drawn film the render is cheap to redo; the expensive thing is effort spent polishing a direction
the director would have rejected. The first notes usually change every plate (environments, acting, tone).

**How to apply:** plan the gate into any film, site or app look. Turn the notes into the scene guide's DIRECTION
section, verbatim where possible, overriding everything below it. When the director says "I trust you, take it to the
final", the gates become milestones: send the boards and the preview, and keep going. See [[feedback-script-first]].
