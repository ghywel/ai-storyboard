# <Film title>: the look

Written before any plate is built or any still is generated (CRAFT.md §1). The anchor frames are judged against it, and
every generated still is checked against its palette (`tools/palette.py`).

## Palette

| Token | Hex | Role | Weight |
|---|---|---|---|
| ground | | the background of most frames | about half of every frame |
| <neutral> | | | |
| accent | | the one thing to look at in a shot, never decoration | a few % |

No new hues later: only changes of value and alpha. (Ask the director about blue-purple gradients and neon cyan-violet
glows: one community project bans them as the generic "AI look"; the Stone films' taste wanted neon on indigo.)

## Medium

What the picture is made of, as a process: <paper and watercolour washes / broken-colour oil / cel with painted
backgrounds / ink on paper / photographic illustration>. The layer order (CRAFT.md §2), and how characters take the
same medium.

## Light

The ambient (how dark the unlit world is), the key light's direction and colour, the separation light, and how the
light changes across the film (night → dawn, for example).

## Grade

`contrast`, `saturation`, `split`, `shadowTint`, `highlightTint`, `splitBalance` (CRAFT.md §4), per film or per act.

## Type (if there are words on screen)

At most one headline family and one body family; the lyric presentation (DIRECTION.md §3–4).
